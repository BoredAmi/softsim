    Validation: predictions on data the model never saw¶

    Fitting a model to data and then showing it matches that data says how well the fit converged, not how good the model is. This page lists only predictions: each result compares a model with data that played no part in building it.

    Check Data the model saw Data it predicts Result
    Drop card → independent tension test the 20 and 50 g drop videos quasi-static tension of Ecoflex 00-10, another lab, optical strain 3–22% up to 100% strain
    Tension → compression tension specimens compression specimens, same lab 4–17%
    Compression → tension compression specimens (to 25%) tension specimens to 100% 4.5–20% (Mold Max 32%)
    Leave one weight out one drop recording the other drop recording weight trajectory 22–28%; the specimen's hop fails (51–93%)
    Labs against each other – the same product measured by different labs up to 3× apart

    Regenerate: python tools/validate_materials.py (sections 2, 3 and 5) and python tools/leave_one_out.py (section 4).

    1. A drop-fitted card predicts an independent tension test¶

    The physical Ecoflex 00-10 card (Neo-Hookean, E0 = 143 kPa, Prony g = 0.69 at τ = 2 ms) was fitted only to the first contact of the 20 and 50 g drop videos, on the locking-free 3D reference (experiments.md). Its fully relaxed response is a prediction for a slow tension test.

    Shojaeifard and Bacca 2025 (arXiv:2506.05371, Table 1) measured Ecoflex 00-10 in quasi-static tension, with optical strain tracking, three specimens: a one-term Ogden law with E = 34 kPa and α = 2.8. Their deep-indentation tests agree with it (E = 38 kPa).

    Nominal stress of each card over theirs:

    Stretch 1.25 1.5 2.0 2.5 3.0
    Physical card from the drop (relaxed) 1.22 1.13 0.97 0.84 0.74
    MuJoCo effective drop fit 1.50 1.38 1.14 0.96 0.81
    Datasheet card (ecoflex_00_10) 2.49 2.28 1.90 1.59 1.35
    Marechal 2021 card (crosshead strain) 0.85 0.72 0.62 0.58 0.56
    • The drop-fitted card predicts it within 3–22% up to 100% strain.
    • Above that it falls short because Neo-Hookean does not stiffen, and the drops never strain the material that far.
    • The datasheet's 100% modulus (55 kPa) is about 2× too stiff for this lab's material. A joint fit of the drop and that datasheet value was therefore stopped: it would have pulled the card the wrong way.

    2. Tension-only cards predict compression¶

    Roels et al. 2025: several tension and compression specimens per material. A card fitted to the tension specimens only (0–100%, law chosen by BIC as in the library) predicts the compression specimens (0 to −25%). Error = RMS / the specimen's largest stress, averaged over specimens:

    Material Law Fit (tension) Predicted (compression) Joint fit, compression
    Dragon Skin 20 Gent 4.7% 13.5% 11.0%
    Dragon Skin 30 Yeoh 1.0% 12.2% 4.9%
    Ecoflex 00-20 Arruda–Boyce 10.8% 7.3% 2.0%
    Ecoflex 00-30 Yeoh 4.1% 5.6% 1.4%
    Ecoflex 00-50 Yeoh 3.6% 5.5% 1.6%
    Mold Max 14NV Yeoh 0.8% 3.9% 3.5%
    Mold Star 30 Yeoh 0.7% 16.7% 5.1%

    3. Compression-only cards predict tension¶

    The reverse is harder. 25% compression barely constrains a law's higher-order terms.

    Material Law Fit (compression) Predicted (tension, to 100%) With Neo-Hookean
    Dragon Skin 20 Arruda–Boyce 10.9% 20.6% 19.3%
    Dragon Skin 30 Arruda–Boyce 4.5% 16.0% 21.2%
    Ecoflex 00-20 Neo-Hookean 0.8% 11.7% 11.7%
    Ecoflex 00-30 Mooney–Rivlin 1.4% 4.5% 10.0%
    Ecoflex 00-50 Neo-Hookean 1.6% 7.8% 7.8%
    Mold Max 14NV Mooney–Rivlin 3.3% 32.0% 18.9%
    Mold Star 30 Arruda–Boyce 4.7% 8.5% 26.0%

    This check found a bug, now fixed. Yeoh fits to compression alone were marked stable, because stability was checked only over the fitted range (up to a stretch of 1). In tension they diverged (errors of 10⁸% and more). Fits are now checked for stability up to a stretch of at least 2, so such laws are rejected. Every card still records the strain range it was fitted on (validity), and softsim check warns when a run leaves it.

    4. Leave one weight out (whole bounce)¶

    The MuJoCo whole-bounce model (stiffness, damping, tack, cloth support: 7 parameters) is refitted to one recording only, from neutral starting values, and then predicts the other recording's first 100 ms (calibrations/loo_ecoflex_00_10_fit_w0x0.yaml, tools/leave_one_out.py).

    Error = NRMSE over the first 100 ms: the mean of the weight's trajectory (indentation) and the specimen's hop (specimen_lift).

    Fitted on Own recording Predicting the other Fitted on both, same recording
    20 g 13% 50 g: 60% (weight 28%, hop 93%) 8%
    50 g 15% 20 g: 37% (weight 22%, hop 51%) 18%

    The material transfers; the setup model does not. - The material agrees between the folds: E = 45 vs 53 kPa, β = 0.87 vs 1.0 ms (the MuJoCo effective values). Each fold also predicts the other weight's trajectory nearly as well as it fits its own (22–28% against 18–20%), and the first-contact peak within 2% (4.39 vs 4.31 mm, fitted on 20 g) and 23% (2.89 vs 3.74 mm, fitted on 50 g). - The cloth support is not identified by one recording. The folds put its mass at 2 g and 77 g, its damping at 1.25 and 0.32 N s/m, and the tack range at 1.2 and 0.3 mm. Each fits its own hop (8–10%), but predicts the other's poorly (51–93%). - So the support and tack values in the drop cards describe the two recordings together. They aren't properties of the material or the table, and the specimen-hop results in experiments.md hold for these two weights, not as predictions for others.

    5. How much do labs disagree?¶

    The same product, measured by different labs (stress ratios as in section 1, against Shojaeifard and Bacca's Ecoflex 00-30: E = 81 kPa, α = 2.4):

    Ecoflex 00-30 card Stretch 1.25 1.5 2.0 3.0
    Roels et al. 2025 (preferred library card) 1.90 1.79 1.57 1.74
    Datasheet card 1.35 1.29 1.16 0.95
    Marechal et al. 2021 (crosshead strain) 0.61 0.59 0.56 0.52

    Small-strain moduli of Ecoflex 00-30 from five sources: 54 kPa (Marechal), 68 kPa (Croquette et al. 2026, 3μ0), 81 kPa (Shojaeifard and Bacca), about 118 kPa (datasheet 100% modulus, Neo-Hookean) and 166 kPa (Roels).

    Batch, cure and test method spread a nominal silicone over a factor of 3. No simulation can be more accurate than that for a part made from an unmeasured batch. For accurate work, measure your own material (one tension test, softsim fit-material). A library card is a starting point with an uncertainty of about ±50%, not a fact.
