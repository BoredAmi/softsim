    Changelog¶

    0.1.0 (2026-11-13)¶

    The first release: a validated core, three robot families and the reference solver they are checked against. See docs/roadmap.md for the details of each milestone.

    • Core (M1): YAML configs with units and strict schemas, --set overrides, material presets, soft and rigid geometry, tetrahedral meshing, a backend-neutral scene, capability flags with refusals, pre-run physics checks, sweeps, CLI.
    • SOFA inverse (--method sofa-qp): angle targets (bend_angle, chord_angle) with any actuator, alone or with position targets (Broyden iteration on the effector goals, angle_direction); a pressure and a cable together (scenarios/inflation/bending_cable.yaml); stroke-controlled cables (solved as tensions, stroke read back, stroke bounds iterated). Round trips recover the actuation to 0.5% or better.
    • SOFA cable (fix): tension-controlled cables are springs with per-step rest lengths, so their tangent stiffness is implicit; the two-cable arm no longer swings in a +-0.5 mm limit cycle on SOFA and the repeated inverse solve converges.
    • Backends: MuJoCo flex (M1), SOFA v26.06 (M4), fem, the locking-free 3D reference (quadratic tetrahedra, P2/P0; M5), and rod, slender bodies as SOFA BeamAdapter beams (M8).
    • Self-collision (M9): body.self_collision for the actuation scenario (MuJoCo flex, SOFA); fem and rod refuse.
    • ROS 2 Humble layer (M2): a generic node, interfaces, launch and RViz.
    • Experiments (M3, M5): drop-test video tracking (including tilt), calibration over any observable, softsim fit against any measured table, leave-one-out validation.
    • Materials (M5): physical and effective cards with validity, uncertainty and references; Gent and Arruda-Boyce; homogeneous tests; fit-material on public data (Marechal 2021, Roels 2025); 25 fitted silicone cards; Prony viscoelasticity on every backend; softsim transfer with error bars; sensitivity and sampling.
    • Gymnasium environments (M12 groundwork): softsim.envs (extra rl), softsim/CableBeamReach-v0 and softsim/CableArmReach-v0, YAML-defined reach tasks with domain randomisation over the cards' uncertainty; softsim/PneuNetBend-v0 (pressure-driven bend angle), softsim/ReducedGripperLift-v0 (grasp and lift on the reduced gripper, randomised object mass and friction) and tools/train_baseline.py (numpy CEM baseline vs random actions).
    • Actuation (M6): pressure, cable (tension or stroke), and region-force actuators; material zones with their own density and damping; bodies from STEP/STL/OBJ/msh; moving and rotating mounts; prescribed region motion; closed-loop control from Python.
    • Robots (M7-M10): PneuNet and the Wang et al. ribbed finger (reference within 0.8 deg RMS of the measurements), cable finger and arm, a two-finger gripper with finite-element grasps and a live page, soft-magnetic elastomers (MRE) on the reference.
    • Benchmarks, full tier: docs/benchmarks.md now has the full tier (hard-magnetic cantilever and the Wang finger: the reference is 0.76 deg RMS from the measurement, MuJoCo and SOFA 18-19 deg, listed as known failures). The 10 g finite-element grasp was re-run with the default friction on the 2 mm mesh (unchanged) and on the validated 1.5 mm finger with teeth (grip while holding 13% higher); the 30 g one stops converging at t = 1.04 s.
    • Drop comparisons: calibrations made before the measured landing tilt was applied (28-29 Oct) are re-simulated without it (tilt: false in the calibration file); with the tilt the SOFA 500 g fits blew up and were missing from the figures. The SOFA Ogden fit's 500 g NRMSE is 79% without the rim artefact (it was reported as 58%).
    • SOFA contact: the alarm distance follows the fastest vertex's travel per step (rim flare of the 500 g drop: a vertex went 2.83 mm through the ground, now 0.006 mm; the restitution table is unchanged, +12% CPU time in that run; SOFTSIM_SOFA_ADAPTIVE_ALARM=0 restores the fixed one). The "frictionless plate 7% stiff" artefact was friction: SOFA's single coefficient (0.1 gives about -7%, 0.8 -33%), at 0 the plate agrees with no contact to 0.6%.
    • Friction: a sliding-block benchmark against exact Coulomb sliding (all backends within 0.8-0.94%) and its sticking case; the reference's friction smoothing is now a slip speed (contact.slip_speed, default 1e-5 m/s), so its stick creep no longer depends on the time step (docs/decisions.md, entry 6). This changes finite-element grasps; null restores the old behaviour.
    • SOFA contact: its "58% energy loss" in frictionless impacts was a friction mismatch (one coefficient for every pair); compared like for like it loses 3.4% more than the reference. Runs now warn (single-friction) when a SOFA scene's coefficients differ.
    • Mounts: a rotating mount can turn at a constant rate (waypoints [time, angle, rate]).
    • Magnetic: saturation of the induced magnetisation (a tanh law from the card's saturation_magnetization) and hard-magnetic particles (remanent_magnetization, Zhao et al. 2019), both on the reference; magnetic zones (each element with its own card) and a hard-magnetic U gripper whose oppositely magnetised arms close in a uniform field.
    • Design and control (M11, first pieces): softsim optimize (design optimisation over parametric geometry, a resumable evaluation log, constraints with an honest infeasible flag) and softsim inverse (quasi-static inverse control: the actuator values that reach target outputs), both checked on MuJoCo; values are the backend's own. softsim inverse --method sofa-qp is the SOFA fast path with SoftRobots.Inverse (QPInverseProblemSolver, CableActuator, PositionEffector): same tensions as the repeated solve to 1%, 7-11 times faster on the cantilever, and it solves the two-cable arm where the repeated solve on SOFA does not settle. Pressure actuators too (SurfacePressureActuator on the cavity, jobs/inverse_bending_sofa.yaml): 4 kPa recovered to 0.03% on the bending tube (7 times faster than the repeated solve), 5 kPa to 0.05% on the PneuNet.
    • Surrogates (M11): softsim surrogate fits a polynomial or RBF model of the equilibrium map from seeded samples (resumable log, held-out validation, JSON evaluable from numpy) and softsim inverse --method surrogate solves on it in milliseconds with an optional verifying simulation; optional DMD-with-control dynamic model. MuJoCo arm: the known tensions found to 0.01%, ~1500 times faster per solve than the repeated solve.
    • Closed-loop control (M11): softsim.control.controller.Controller (surrogate feedforward, damped-least-squares integral feedback with anti-windup, optional MPC-lite on the DMD model) and a ROS 2 controller_node; with a stiffness 20 % off, feedback removes the 17 to 33 % (cantilever) and 1 to 3 mm (arm) feedforward error on MuJoCo.
    • Runs: a run that fails partway saves the samples it computed (summary.yaml → metadata.incomplete).
    • Release (M13): softsim bench and the benchmark table, CI, a docs site with tutorials, a test that every command in the README and tutorials parses and its config validates, softsim --version.
