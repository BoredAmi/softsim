# Benchmarks

Every validated case on every backend against its truth (an analytic solution or a measurement),
generated by `softsim bench`. The tolerance is the error bar softsim states for that backend on that
case, so *pass* means *within the documented error bar*, not *exact*: MuJoCo and SOFA use linear
tetrahedra, which lock in bending ([actuation.md](actuation.md)); the `fem` reference is locking-free.

Regenerate: `softsim bench --tier quick` (minutes) or `--tier full` (hours; GPU machine), then
`softsim bench --report-only`. A row marked *stale* shows a result from an older config or older code: re-run it.

## Cases

- **cable_cantilever**: 10 x 10 x 60 mm beam (E 500 kPa) bent by a cable 3 mm off its axis, anchored 5 mm below the top, 0.7 N: a pure moment, so Euler-Bernoulli gives the tip rotation exactly at any angle (with the Neo-Hookean tangent under the cable's pre-compression). Notes: rod: a materially linear beam, so it misses the 2.8% Neo-Hookean stiffening under the cable pre-compression (2T/EA); mujoco: linear tetrahedra lock in bending; sofa: linear tetrahedra lock in bending.
- **hard_magnetic_cantilever**: A 4 x 4 x 60 mm bar magnetised along its length (100 kA/m, hard-magnetic) in a 30 A/m field across it: the couple mu0 M_r x H bends it by Euler-Bernoulli's c L^3 / (3 E I), c = mu0 M_r H A. Reference only (MuJoCo and SOFA have no magnetism). Notes: fem: +1.1% at a 1 mm mesh in tests/test_magnetic.py (second order).
- **self_collision**: A two-link cable finger (Ecoflex 00-30, one 2 mm slot) whose tendon pulls the slot shut: with self-collision on, the slot's two walls must not pass through each other. The quantity is the smallest signed gap between the walls over the run (negative: one inside the other); the truth is contact, a gap of zero, and the tolerance the contact skin. Without self-collision the walls pass 1.3-1.4 mm through each other (tests/test_self_collision.py). Notes: mujoco: the gap left is MuJoCo's contact margin; sofa: the gap left is the contact distance.
- **sliding_block**: A 40 x 40 x 10 mm silicone block (17.1 g) on the ground, friction 0.5, pushed by 0.12 N spread over its top face (ramped over 50 ms, then held): Coulomb sliding, a = (F - mu m g) / m. The travel after 300 ms against the rigid block's exact travel (the block's own shear adds about 0.01 mm).
- **sticking_block**: The same block pushed by 0.06 N, 0.71 of the friction limit (0.084 N): Coulomb says it sticks, so the top face moves only by the block's shear (about 0.005 mm). MuJoCo's friction is a soft constraint and creeps at a steady rate instead (about 0.13 mm/s here), which is what a held grasp does on MuJoCo; the tolerance is that creep over 300 ms. Notes: fem: creeps at about 0.005 mm/s with the default contact.slip_speed (1e-5 m/s); the old per-step smoothing (slip_speed null) crept 0.233 mm/s at 2 ms (docs/decisions.md, entry 6); mujoco: soft-constraint friction creeps at about 0.13 mm/s while sticking.
- **tension_test**: Roller tension test, 10 x 10 x 20 mm Neo-Hookean bar (E 100 kPa, nu 0.45) pulled 10% by prescribed motion; the pull at the hold is the homogeneous uniaxial stress times the area, which linear and quadratic tetrahedra both represent exactly. MuJoCo and SOFA run the stable Neo-Hookean of Smith et al., 0.9% stiffer at 10%. Notes: mujoco: stable Neo-Hookean is 0.9% stiffer than the split form at 10%; sofa: stable Neo-Hookean is 0.9% stiffer than the split form at 10%.
- **tip_load_cantilever**: The same beam under a 5 mN dead load along +x spread over its top face. Euler-Bernoulli F L^3 / (3 E I) = 0.864 mm; the reference converges to it (Timoshenko's 2.4% shear is offset by the fully clamped root). Notes: mujoco: linear tetrahedra lock in bending; sofa: linear tetrahedra lock in bending.
- **wang_finger**: Cast ribbed pneumatic finger of Wang et al., Actuators 15(9), 488 (2026), CC BY 4.0: geometry from the paper's Abaqus mesh, Yeoh card from its tensile data, plane strain as in the paper; chord angle against the eight measured points, 5-40 kPa (one finger, one ascending sequence; reading uncertainty about 1 deg). Notes: mujoco: does not reproduce this finger at 1-2 mm (8-19 deg RMS, docs/actuation.md); sofa: does not reproduce this finger at 1-2 mm (8-19 deg RMS, docs/actuation.md).
